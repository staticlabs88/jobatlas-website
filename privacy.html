Privacy policy

Effective September 24, 2026 · JobAtlas Android testing release and informational website

JobAtlas helps people find work, organize applications and publish jobs, gigs and services. Contact JobAtlas support at miker9926@gmail.com about this policy or your data.

Information on your device

Resumes, cover letters, saved contacts, references, application tracking and preferences are stored locally by the app. They are not automatically uploaded as a cloud resume library. Files you export or share can include personal information; you choose where they go. Protect your device and backups.

Accounts and Google sign-in

If you create an online account, our authentication provider, Supabase, processes your email, account identity and sign-in credentials. Google sign-in, when enabled for your account, uses your basic Google profile and email to authenticate you. JobAtlas does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive or Google Contacts. Authentication tokens are kept in the device’s secure storage.

We use account information to sign you in and associate your online listings and account activity with you. We do not sell Google account data or use it for targeted advertising.

Location and searches

You can select a location manually. If you choose the device-location feature and grant permission, location is used to help select a nearby search area. The app remembers your selected location. You can change it in Search or revoke location permission in Android settings.

Search terms, selected location and filters are sent to the relevant job sources and our search backend to return listings. Location lookup providers may receive coordinates when you use that feature. Network services also receive technical information such as your IP address. JobAtlas does not require continuous background location tracking.

Public listings and community content

Jobs, gigs, service advertisements, reviews and the contact information you choose to publish may be visible to other users after review. Reports and account information are used to manage abuse and support requests. Do not publish private information about another person without permission.

Service providers and external sites

Supabase provides shared data storage and authentication. GitHub Actions runs the public job-index crawler. Hosting and network providers process technical requests and may keep operational logs. Data may be processed outside your country. When you follow an employer’s application link, that website handles the information you submit under its own privacy policy. JobAtlas does not control those sites.

Payments and tracking

Paid features are not active in this testing release. If Google Play purchases are enabled later, purchase handling and verification will be described before launch. The current app and this informational website do not include an advertising or behavioral analytics SDK.

Retention and security

Local information remains on your device until you remove it, clear app data or uninstall, subject to backups you keep. Online account information and published content are retained while needed for the account and service, unless deleted. Providers may retain operational backups and logs under their retention practices. We use access controls and encrypted network connections, but cannot promise absolute security.

Delete your account or request data removal

Email miker9926@gmail.com from your account email with the subject “JobAtlas account deletion”. Identify the account or public listing to remove. We may ask you to verify account ownership; never send your password.

You can also request access, correction or removal of specific online information without deleting the entire account. Account deletion removes your authentication account and associated owned online listings, reviews and reports from the active service. Provider backups and operational logs may not disappear immediately. Local resumes, exported files and backups are separate: delete these on your device or wherever you saved them. Information submitted directly to employers must be managed with those employers.

Changes

We will update this page when data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version. Contact support if you need help understanding a change.
